## FT-001 findings

The test set a disconfirming condition before analysis: if the journey found nothing beyond Alchemy's own documentation, it would add no analytic value over an ordinary repository review.

The condition was partially defeated. The journey surfaced several readings not already explicit in Alchemy's documentation, especially through the extraction check. It also repeated two findings the practice already knew. The additional value was smaller than the length of the walk might suggest.

The test recorded these method failures and tensions:

- An AI assessor could not perform the body-based Observe station. That station ran as design analysis and needs a human redo.
- No second reader was available.
- The assessor chose the seven-surface boundary. A different boundary would change the denominators.
- The traceable construct treated provenance as a virtue while Alchemy used intentional deletion as a design feature. The instrument has no category for accountable ephemerality.
- The review came from inside the maintainer's orbit and may have scored the practice generously.

## Adaptation and follow-up

FT-001 proposed an inbound route for field reports and corrections in the Alchemy repository. The owner approved and executed the change through [Alchemy pull request 15](https://github.com/risaac09/alchemy/pull/15), merged as `bf5e411`.

The observation window runs to 2026-11-22. The follow-up evidence is the count of inbound reports and the burden of handling them. No causal result is available.
