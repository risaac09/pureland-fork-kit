# Hypothesis

## Primary hypothesis H-PRIMARY-0.1

For a named walking person in a bounded information practice, a PureLand-guided adaptation can produce a material increase in practical agency and attention sovereignty for one or more predefined actions, while preserving a usable route to contest or refuse the practice and without a material increase in exposure, extractability, or shifted burden for any affected person or group. The practice should also return something the affected person recognizes as meaningful.

This is a provisional, context-bound hypothesis. No current measure is validated.

## Version 0.1 constructs

| Construct | Provisional definition | Evidence needed in a test | What does not establish it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Practical agency | A person's ability to carry out a predefined action within the tested boundary without an unreasonable permission, information, time, money, or coordination barrier. | A named action, baseline, follow-up, observation method, and the person's result. | An artifact count or the assessor's sense that the system is better. |
| Attention sovereignty | A person's practical ability to direct, pause, resume, or end contact while understanding the contact's state and consequences, without a manufactured penalty for stopping. | Baseline and follow-up for a relevant action such as stop, continue, question, or refuse, plus human observation or report. | Interface features alone, an AI design reading, or a document-access count. |
| Exposure | Information, identity, access, or protected knowledge becoming observable to more actors, for more uses, or for longer than the affected party authorized. | Baseline and follow-up for every affected party, including recipients, uses, retention, and evidence gaps. | The absence of a reported incident. |
| Extractability | The practical ease with which another actor can capture, combine, transfer, or reuse a person's contribution outside its intended purpose or permission. | Baseline and follow-up for access, copying, combination, reuse rights, and technical or social friction. | Public availability by itself, whether judged favorable or unfavorable. |
| Shifted burden | Time, cost, risk, monitoring, correction work, or relational labor moved from the practitioner or institution to an affected party because of the practice or adaptation. | Baseline and follow-up by affected party, with the kind and bearer of the burden named. | Total effort without showing who bears it. |
| Material increase | A change large enough, under a threshold stated before follow-up, to alter an affected party's action, rights, risk, or required effort in the tested context. | A context-specific threshold or decision rule, baseline, follow-up, and evidence. | Any numerical movement, or a threshold chosen after seeing the result. |
| Contestability | An affected person can understand the relevant decision, reach a correction or refusal route, use it without unreasonable burden or retaliation, and receive a bounded response. | A traced route, its permission boundary, an attempted action or justified unmeasurable status, and response evidence when the window closes. | A contact address that no affected person tried, or an assessor's ability to file an issue. |
| Meaningful return | Something comes back in a timely and usable form that the affected person recognizes as relevant to what they contributed and proportionate to the lasting value the practice keeps. | The affected person's account or exercise of the return, plus what left, what returned, when, and under whose decision. | Publication, access, or an assessor-declared benefit by itself. |

These definitions make the hypothesis testable. They do not establish reliability, validity, generalizability, or a causal effect.

## Classification rule

Classify the tested context only. Do not average readings or calculate a composite.

- **Support for the tested context:** all required person-level outcome and rights evidence is present; at least one predefined action shows a material increase in practical agency or attention sovereignty; the correction or refusal route is usable; no affected party shows a material increase in exposure, extractability, or shifted burden; and meaningful return is evidenced.
- **Unmeasurable:** any required outcome evidence or permission-and-rights evidence is missing. Keep any measured access or reciprocity readings, but do not convert them into support.
- **Weakened:** a material increase in exposure, extractability, or shifted burden appears but does not make the adaptation untenable, or the correction or refusal route is not usable. An absent or assessor-only meaningful return also weakens the hypothesis when the other required evidence is measurable.
- **Defeated for the tested context:** a material increase in exposure, extractability, or shifted burden makes the adaptation untenable under the predefined condition, or the adaptation removes practical agency or attention sovereignty for a predefined action.
- **Mixed:** the evidence supports some constructs and weakens or defeats others. Keep the profile mixed. Do not net benefits against harms.

No classification extends beyond the named practice, people, boundary, actions, and observation window. No before-and-after impression becomes a causal claim.
