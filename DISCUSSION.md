# Discussion

I have tested the journey once, on Alchemy, a tool I own, with an AI assessor working for me. That proximity cannot support an outcome claim. It can show me where the method fails before I ask someone outside the project to carry its burden.

## FT-001 in this record

The extraction check produced most of the new analysis in the first worked example. The full journey may still help a first-time practitioner complete the work, but FT-001 does not show that its sequence adds enough value to justify the extra burden. That question needs a comparison between the full journey and a smaller entry through one instrument.

The Observe station requires a human first-person register. An AI system can analyze design evidence but cannot report a bodily experience. Future records should mark the station incomplete when no human performs it instead of substituting design analysis under the same label.

Traceability and deletion need separation. A system can lose provenance through neglect, or its owner can delete material under a visible rule. Those are different conditions. The scorecard needs a way to record accountable ephemerality without granting deletion a favorable result by default.

Bounding is part of measurement. Counts change when the assessor changes the unit or set. A field report should explain why its boundary is appropriate and show how a plausible alternative boundary would change the reading when that choice is consequential.

## Competing explanations

The new readings in FT-001 may come from the method. They may also come from the assessor's familiarity with both repositories, the amount of time spent, or the extraction check's vocabulary alone. The current record cannot separate those explanations.

The favorable access and reciprocity readings may describe Alchemy accurately. They may also reflect maintainer-side evidence selection, the absence of affected-user testimony, and the lack of a second reader.

## Revisions indicated by the evidence

1. Require a human for the Observe station and record the station as incomplete otherwise.
2. Add boundary rationale and sensitivity to the field-test record.
3. Distinguish accountable deletion from missing provenance in the traceable construct.
4. Compare the full journey with a single-instrument path before claiming sequencing value.
5. Prioritize independent applications and second-reader disagreement over more maintainer-side examples.
6. Test whether the applied [PureLand](PURELAND.md) experience reduces entry burden before expanding it.

These are revision proposals. The instruments do not contain them, and nobody has tested them.

## Rights and publication

More evidence can increase exposure without improving the inquiry. Independent testing cannot require participant footage, public transcripts, identifying data, or protected knowledge. A private refusal, an aggregate record, or an `unmeasurable` field can be the evidence-compatible result.
