# Contributing

PureLand needs documented disagreement more than applause. Refusal and private learning are valid outcomes. No one owes the project a public field report.

## Useful contributions

- a public-safe field test that uses only material cleared for the actual report;
- a case where a category failed or caused harm;
- a correction to a source or claim;
- a clearer consent boundary;
- an accessibility or translation improvement;
- an adaptation that states what changed and why; or
- a dissenting interpretation that the contributor has authority to publish.

Do not submit participant material, identifying or indirectly identifying information, client or confidential records, protected community knowledge, completed consent registers, or content you lack authority to license. Anonymizing material does not make it public-safe when combinations of details create re-identification risk.

## Public field-test scoping

Use the [field-test issue form](https://github.com/risaac09/pureland-fork-kit/issues/new?template=field-test.yml) for Stage 1. Describe the practice in general terms, flag the kinds of protected material that may exist, and state what must remain private. Do not link participant evidence or describe protected content.

Stage 2 begins only after the maintainer establishes an appropriate private review channel and confirms the rights basis. The repository names no default private system. No participant or protected material should enter a public issue, pull request, repository record, or AI system while the channel or authority remains unresolved. See [RIGHTS-AND-CONSENT.md](RIGHTS-AND-CONSENT.md).

## Pull-request process

1. Start from the current main branch.
2. Make one bounded change.
3. Update the relevant provenance or research-status entry when a claim or source changes.
4. Complete the pull-request template, including affected people, rights basis, exposure or burden, disagreement, public-safe review, and claim status.
5. Run `python3 scripts/check_repo.py`.
6. Submit only the material cleared for public release.

Keep the consent register and protected review evidence private. A public-safe conclusion needs a documented review; a checkbox alone does not establish one.

By submitting original repository content, you agree to license that contribution under CC BY-SA 4.0. This covers only material you have authority to license. Participant approval to publish an artifact does not by itself grant an open license.

Maintainer decisions follow [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). Critique the method and decisions. Do not diagnose contributors with contemplative categories or attack anyone's identity. When a dispute requires protected detail, request a private review channel without posting the detail.
