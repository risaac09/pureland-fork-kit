01 / The method

Begin with one
real practice.

Choose an information practice of your own. Work through six steps, in order, and keep a record of what you can and cannot establish.

You may stop at any step. Recording is optional. A stopped run cannot support claims about steps nobody completed.

Read the full method ↗
A place to begin

You could examine your own use of a language model: the material you share with it and the control you retain over that material.

IScopeName the practice and its boundary.

Name who the practice affects. Record what evidence exists, and one result that would count against the method.

Before a test ↗
IIAttendNotice your own attention and body.

Run the practice frame before assigning a condition to the system. These are prompts for self-observation, never scores or labels for other people. Attend belongs to the person; a model cannot perform it.

Open the practice frame ↗
IIIReadExamine access in four separate readings.

Ask whether the material is understandable, reachable, adaptable, and traceable. Name the unit, set, denominator, and time window before counting. Keep the four measures separate.

Open the access scorecard ↗
IVTraceFollow what leaves and what returns.

Trace information from each contributor to where it accumulates. Read consent, attribution, and value return separately. Do not average the bands or produce a combined score.

Open the extraction check ↗
VAdaptTry one change and watch its cost.

Record the baseline, intended benefit, plausible new harm, required consent, and review date. Observe the cost as well as the result.

Read the adaptation guidance ↗
VIReportKeep the result within its evidence.

Record what happened, including disagreement or an unmeasurable result. Private learning and refusal may end without a public report. Only cleared reports enter the public ledger. One reply from the maintainer is there for the asking, at no cost.

02 / Current evidence

An open record.
An unanswered question.

PureLand has one maintainer-side, AI-assisted partial dry run and no independent field trial. The current evidence does not show that the method works.

The instruments have no reliability or validity result. Missing evidence stays unmeasurable.

What informs PureLand?

The synthesis draws on Buddhist practice, evaluation, body-based practice, and technology theory. Its mappings are original synthesis, not Buddhist doctrine, clinical categories, or validated measures. The name establishes no lineage relationship to Pure Land Buddhist traditions. Read the thesis and the provenance record.

Permission stays specific

Open work has boundaries.

The repository contains methods, templates, and public-safe records. Participant material stays outside it. Recording, retention, reviewer access, publication, research use, AI processing, and model training require separate decisions.

A repository license does not grant participant permission. Withdrawal may stop future uses the project controls; it may not retrieve copies already distributed.
