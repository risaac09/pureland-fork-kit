# Conclusion

PureLand is a testable research program with a forkable toolbox. I have one worked example from inside my own orbit. The current evidence does not show that the method works.

The current record supports a narrow conclusion:

> The thesis is coherent enough to produce a method, instruments, disconfirming conditions, and inspectable failures. The first worked example found some additional analytic value and several problems that require revision. No independent or causal conclusion is available.

The forkable toolbox remains necessary because it makes the work inspectable and adaptable. It remains insufficient because attention sovereignty also depends on consent, rights, burden, return, evidence, governance, and the ability of affected people to challenge the reading.

## Next evidence

The next useful move is not a stronger claim. It is independent use:

- five to ten applications across at least three contexts;
- two readers where practical;
- a human completion of the Observe station;
- explicit boundary rationale;
- follow-up on the tested adaptation;
- public records only where rights and consent permit.

## Conditions for changing the conclusion

The conclusion should strengthen only if independent tests show that the method produces useful, contestable changes without shifting exposure or burden onto affected people. It should weaken if the journey adds little beyond ordinary review, the constructs do not survive second readers, the work increases extractability, or the return is not meaningful to the people who generated the value.

This conclusion is provisional. It should move when [results](RESULTS.md) move.
