# Consent register

Store this record privately with access limited to the people who need it. Do not commit a completed register to the public repository. This template records decisions for one participant or other rights holder. It is not universal legal language.

Use `yes`, `no`, or `not yet` for each decision. Add conditions and the basis for authority. A yes in one row does not imply a yes anywhere else.
