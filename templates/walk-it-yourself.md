# Walk it yourself

## What this is

A plain Markdown worksheet for one person walking the six stations of [the journey](../JOURNEY.md) on one bounded information practice. It links to the instruments that define the work. Keep those linked files open and record your answers here.

This packet adds no score, claim, or interpretation. [CURRENT-EVIDENCE.md](../CURRENT-EVIDENCE.md) caps what PureLand has shown.

## Before you start

Choose one practice that you take part in and that affects your information, attention, work, or relationships. Set aside enough time to stop between Observe and Adapt. The observation window must pass before you close the follow-up.

Read the [five measurement units](../METHOD.md#measurement-units). Name each unit below before analysis.

Keep participant, client, confidential, identifying, and protected community material out of public issues and any model session that lacks approval for that material and use. A public Stage 1 issue accepts general scoping only. Follow [RIGHTS-AND-CONSENT.md](../RIGHTS-AND-CONSENT.md) and [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md) before moving protected material.

Choose one ending you can use without pressure: public return, private learning, refusal, or `unmeasurable`. [The path-ending rule](../JOURNEY.md#when-the-path-ends) controls any completion claim.

## After the walk

Ask someone affected by the practice to challenge the reading when rights permit. Preserve their correction or disagreement. Revisit the review date after the observation window and record what changed, what did not, and what remains missing.

Run `python3 scripts/check_repo.py` only if you create repository artifacts. The checker tests structure and cross-file consistency. It does not establish completion, validity, safety, or outcome.

## What this packet cannot do

The packet cannot supply your observation, another person's permission, an affected person's challenge, an independent reading, elapsed follow-up time, or a rights decision. It cannot turn a complete worksheet into evidence that PureLand works. Stop when a required person, permission, record, or observation is missing, and use the ending that fits the record you have.
