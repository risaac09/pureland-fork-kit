# Synthetic example: the Millbrook Porch Tapes

Everything here is invented. It demonstrates the instruments and makes no research claim.

## Context

A five-person collective has recorded eight conversations with elders in a mill town. It has published two edited conversations and two essays. The archive helped the collective receive a grant.

## Consent trace

For conversation seven, the participant agreed to audio recording, an edited audio publication after review, and a written companion. He declined video, model training, and future quotation without a new request. His daughter received a raw copy. The collective retained its controlled raw copy for one year. A neighbor's name was removed during review.

## Scorecard

The set is twelve artifacts during the two-year project: eight edited conversations, two essays, a protocol, and a prior internal review.

| Access | Finding | Evidence | Disagreement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Legibility | 5 of 12 reachable artifacts have plain-language descriptions | Public index captured on the review date | Reader two counted a sixth description as legible |
| Permeability | 6 of 12 are publicly reachable; one email route receives corrections | Access test and message log | Response time is unmeasurable because timestamps were not retained |
| Forkability | 2 of 12 have explicit reuse permission; 1 has usable adaptation instructions | License and documentation review | None |
| Provenance | 24 claims verified, 3 partly verified, 4 unverified, 2 intentionally anonymous | Claim ledger | One anonymous source lacks an independent steward |

No composite is calculated.

## Extraction profile

| Dimension | Band | Evidence | Burden or disagreement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Consent | Usable | Separate choices, artifact review, one honored withdrawal | The paper form was difficult for two elders |
| Attribution | Formal | Credits exist on the collective's site | A radio rebroadcast severed the elder's credit |
| Value return | Formal | Families receive copies | Grant money and durable reputation remain with the collective |

The profile shows that careful consent does not settle value return. It also avoids pretending that three judgments can become a validated extraction number.

## Attention and adaptation

The collective notices greed in its wish to keep every raw file and doubt in its repeated postponement of a retention decision. This is the collective's self-observation, not a diagnosis of the elders.

It tests one change: delete controlled raw files at the agreed date unless the participant renews permission. The participant receives notice and a copy first. The collective records losses, exceptions, and whether the new process shifts work onto families.
