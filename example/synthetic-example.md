# Worked Example: The Millbrook Porch Tapes

*(Pure)Land fork kit, component 4 of 5.*

**Everything in this file is synthetic.** The collective, the town, the people, and every number are invented to show the instruments filled in. No participant material exists behind this example. If you recognize your town in it, that is because the shape is common, which is the point.

## The practice

The Porch Tapes are a fictional five-member collective in a fictional New England mill town, recording long conversations with elders on their own porches: work at the mill, the flood, who married whom, what the river smelled like before the remediation. Eight conversations recorded over two years. Two essays published from them. A small grant received on the strength of the archive.

## Protocol trace (one encounter)

Following [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md) for conversation 7, with an 86-year-old former loom fixer:

- **Thread named:** not "mill history" but "what your hands still remember." Questions to live with, not ask.
- **Pre-conversation** on the porch, no recorder, forty minutes. He set one boundary unprompted: nothing about his late son.
- **Consent registered** on one page, read aloud at his request: record yes, publish audio yes, publish video no camera present, companion text yes with review, clips yes with review, future quotes with a heads-up. File terms: his daughter holds a full copy of the raw audio.
- **During:** the recorder went on twenty minutes into a conversation already moving. The named boundary held without being touched.
- **After:** a letter within 48 hours about what the phrase "the loom tells you before it breaks" did to the listener's week. He reviewed the edited cut on the same porch and cut one neighbor's name from it. The cut was made before anything shipped.

## Scorecard reading

Named set (denominator, decided first): the 12 artifacts the collective has produced: 8 edited conversations, 2 essays, the protocol document, 1 prior scorecard reading.

| Access | Reading | Notes |
|---|---|---|

| Permeability | **6/12 reachable; deepest path 3 tiers, 2 boundaries** | Public episode, then members' shared drive, then originals held by each elder's family. Inbound: requests reach one member's personal email; median answer, 11 days |
| Forkability | **2/12** | The protocol doc (CC BY-SA 4.0) and one transcript (CC BY 4.0, released at the elder's own request under tier-3 consent). The 3 other public artifacts carry no license: readable, not forkable |

The four readings stay separate. No composite.

| Member field notes | Nobody decided; drafts by default. Flagged for an actual decision |

The honest entry is the second one. "Closed by default, undecided" recorded as itself, not dressed up as policy.

## Extraction check

- **C_r = 0.8.** Six-ask consent, read aloud when asked, withdrawal honored once in full (conversation 4 was pulled after a family conflict; nothing new derived since).
- **A_r = 0.6.** Credit is solid on the episodes, but a regional radio show re-aired a clip crediting only the collective, not the elder. The attribution severed at the first hop, and the collective did not chase it.
- **V_r = 0.4.** Families hold full copies, which is real. But the grant arrived on the strength of the archive, and none of it reached the people the archive is made of. The durable asset accumulated with the collective; the elders got visibility.

**E_c = 1 / (0.8 x 0.6 x 0.4) = 5.2. Extractive tendency**, driven by V_r, in a practice with warm consent and good manners. That combination is the common one, and it is exactly what the check exists to catch: consent hygiene up front, value quietly pooling at the practice's end of the table.

**Sovereignty paradox check:** the consent process cost the eldest participants the most (a two-page form, small print, a signature culture some found stressful). Fix already applied in the trace above: one page, read aloud, on the porch.

**Collective lens:** partial. Individual consent is strong; the town as a collective controls nothing. The neighbor-names problem in the raw tapes is a collective-consent problem individual paperwork cannot solve, which is a correct reason those tapes stay closed.

**Intervention, weakest reciprocity first:** a written share of any future grant or licensing income to the families (raises V_r), and a re-air agreement template that makes elder credit a condition of rebroadcast (raises A_r). Re-run the check after both, not instead of them.

## What the example is for

Copy the shapes, not the numbers. Your denominators, boundaries, and failures will be your own. The only transferable findings are the method's: decide the denominator first, keep the four accesses separate, write the closure reasons down before anyone asks, score your own reciprocities stingily, and let "unmeasurable" stand wherever the record does not exist.
