# PureLand Field Pilot

The present offering is a bounded co-inquiry into one real information practice. It walks [the journey](JOURNEY.md), the kit's one curated path, on the collaborator's practice together. It is a field pilot, not a mature consultancy, certification, treatment, audit clearance, or guaranteed transformation.

The future consultancy is an application layer on a 10-to-15-year horizon. The present phase is external criticism and bounded field proof.

## Who the pilot is for

The pilot is for a person or small group responsible for, participating in, or affected by an information practice. Examples include facilitation, participatory media, evaluation, community archives, organizational knowledge, or an AI-enabled workflow. The practice must be specific enough to bound and the collaborator must be able to identify what can be inspected and what must remain private.

There are no published eligibility rules, certification requirements, or guaranteed outcomes.

## What the collaborator brings

- one real practice, encounter, or information system;
- the boundary and affected people as currently understood;
- access to evidence that can be reviewed lawfully;
- the doubts, burdens, or disagreements already visible; and
- authority to discuss the practice, or a clear account of whose permission is still needed.

## What Isaac brings

- a bounded inquiry frame;
- [the journey](JOURNEY.md), walked in order at the depth the practice needs;
- consent, rights, attribution, and value-return questions;
- a public/private boundary review;
- a second-reader arrangement where practical; and
- a record of failure, disagreement, refusal, or method revision rather than an endorsement.

## What happens together

1. Name the question, system boundary, affected people, evidence, and one result that would count against the method.
2. Establish the consent and rights needed for the inquiry. Recording is optional.
3. Notice what happens in attention and in the body, using the practice frame.
4. Read the practice through the four accesses and the extraction check.
5. Choose one bounded adaptation and name a plausible new harm.
6. Observe the burden, disagreement, and result.
7. Decide what learning can return publicly and what must remain private.

## What the pilot may produce

Expected outputs may include:

- a defined system boundary;
- a consent and rights plan;
- a four-access profile;
- an extraction and reciprocity profile;
- one bounded adaptation;
- a documented correction, disagreement, refusal, or method revision; and
- a public-safe field report only when consent and rights permit.

The pilot does not require participant recordings, transcripts, names, or film production. No participant material belongs in the public repository by default.

## What remains private

Participant and client material, consent and relationship records, personal notes, protected community knowledge, credentials, identifying data, confidential records, and third-party material without clear reuse rights remain outside the public kit. Withdrawal limits must be stated before collection. See [RIGHTS-AND-CONSENT.md](RIGHTS-AND-CONSENT.md).

## What the pilot does not claim

The pilot does not claim validation, causal proof, safety certification, attention sovereignty as an outcome, a moral verdict, a composite openness score, or a general finding from one case. It does not promise pricing, duration, session count, certification, guaranteed outcomes, or a default film deliverable. Commercial structure and other future consultancy details remain unresolved.

## How to propose a pilot

Open an [issue](https://github.com/risaac09/pureland-fork-kit/issues) with a public-safe note containing:

- the practice and why the question matters now;
- the boundary and affected people;
- the evidence that may be available;
- what must remain private; and
- one result that would count against the method.

Do not include participant material, identifying data, confidential records, or protected community knowledge. A proposal is an invitation to scope a pilot, not an acceptance or a promise of work.
