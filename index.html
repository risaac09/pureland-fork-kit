PureLand

an open method for information practices, v0.1 research prototype
brief journey offering repository issues

Can a way of handling information give you more control over your own attention without also making you easier to exploit?

That is the question PureLand tests, not a claim it has already answered. It combines contemplative self-observation, information-practice analysis, bounded adaptation, and accountable return.

  • give people more control over their attention
  • give people more useful access without exposing them or making them easier to mine
  • build permission, credit, and fair return into how information gets handled
  • keep the practice open to challenge, so the people it affects can question it, change it, or refuse it
  • give reusable methods and rights-cleared research back to a shared commons

What it studies

Measurement without attention or relationship can turn into one more machine for taking things from people. Contemplative and body-based practice without evidence can harden into private conviction. Information theory without permission, credit, and return can describe how data moves while ignoring who pays for the movement.

PureLand draws on Buddhist practice, information theory, evaluation, and body-based practice. It does not claim that Buddhist categories are technical taxonomies, or that Shannon's theory explains meaning or ethics.

Open source is the release phase of the practice, not the whole practice.

Generosity means increasing another person's ability to understand, adapt, contest, or refuse. It does not mean publishing everything. Participant material, protected knowledge, and relationship records may need to remain private.

adaptable

“Accepting and investigating the reality of change.” One of the four access questions PureLand reads a practice through: is it understandable, reachable, adaptable, traceable?

The method

The method walks in six ordered stations, from naming the practice to returning what was learned. Recording is optional. The first pilot does not require participant recordings.

I
Ground

Name the practice, the boundary, and the people it affects. Write one result that would count against the method.

II
Observe

Run the practice frame. Notice what's true in you before assigning it to the system.

III
Map

Score the four access questions with the openness scorecard.

IV
Trace

Run the extraction check. Trace what leaves, where it accumulates, and what returns.

V
Adapt

Choose one bounded change. Watch the cost and the result.

VI
Return

Write up what happened and submit it as a field report.

The six stations of the journey, walked in order on one real practice.

precedent

The closest identified precedent for combining participatory media and evaluation is InsightShare's Participatory Video and the Most Significant Change. PureLand does not claim to originate that combination.

The fork travels both ways

A fork is judged by both directions of travel. Code and method can leave the origin. Something, learning, credit, correction, a returned report, has to travel back for the fork to be more than extraction with a license file attached.

leaves the origin

Walk the journey, the kit's one curated path. Fork the instruments. Submit a public-safe field test. Document a failure or refusal. Propose a correction through the contribution process.

returns

A public-safe field report when permitted, plus a correction, disagreement, refusal, or method revision, recorded in the field-trial ledger, not smoothed over.

license

What remains untested

The instruments have no independent field trial, reliability estimate, validity result, benchmark, threshold, certification, or outcome estimate. The field-trial ledger holds one worked example from the maintainer's side and no independent trial. The next research gate is five to ten independent applications across at least three contexts, with two readers where practical.

The project is currently maintained by Isaac Rubinstein, who holds merge and release authority. That concentration is a project risk, not a mature commons.

correction, recorded

The unvalidated extraction coefficient and composite thresholds were retired. Missing evidence stays unmeasurable; there is no composite score.

Where this leaves you

This page does not ask you to stay. Bring one real practice, a boundary, a doubt, or a place where access and extraction pull against each other, and PureLand will scope what can be inspected, what must remain private, and what result would count against the method. Or don't. The record is here either way, and scroll position on this page earns you nothing.

When you're done reading, close the tab and go look at an actual horizon.
