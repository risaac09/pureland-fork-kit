Can a way of handling information give you more control over your own attention without also making you easier to exploit?

That is the question PureLand tests, not a claim it has already answered. This is version 0.1. The instruments are unvalidated. One maintainer-side partial dry run exists, and no independent field result.

walk the journey the record

what it aims at
  • give people more control over their attention
  • give people more useful access without exposing them or making them easier to mine
  • build permission, credit, and fair return into how information gets handled
  • keep the practice open to challenge, so the people it affects can question it, change it, or refuse it
  • give reusable methods and rights-cleared research back to a shared commons

What it studies

PureLand is a synthesis informed by Buddhist practice, evaluation, body-based practice, and technology theory. The project's mappings are original synthesis. They are not Buddhist doctrine, clinical categories, or validated measures.

Measurement without attention or relationship can turn into one more machine for taking things from people. Contemplative and body-based practice without evidence can harden into private conviction. A map of information movement without permission, credit, and return can ignore who pays for that movement. A reading of a technology's design without the people it lands on can mistake a published architecture for an accountable one. That is why the thesis binds them together rather than picking one.

Open source is the release phase of the practice, not the whole practice.

The project name establishes no lineage relationship to Pure Land Buddhist traditions. The mark diagrams the papañca causal sequence PRACTICE-FRAME.md cites. It replaced an earlier adapted ensō, retired along with the Zen association it carried.

One identified precedent for combining participatory media and evaluation is InsightShare's Participatory Video and the Most Significant Change. PureLand does not claim to originate that combination. PROVENANCE.md holds the sources.

    Prompts for noticing conditions that shape attention and response.

    A technical taxonomy, clinical diagnosis, or label for other people

  • Evaluation

The journey

Six ordered stations, walked on one real practice of your own. Recording is optional. The first pilot does not require participant recordings. You may stop at any station, and a stopped walk cannot support claims about stations nobody completed.

JOURNEY.md holds the full path. The journey stations are distinct from the public research functions above.

I

Ground

Name the practice, the boundary, and the people it affects. Record what evidence exists before you score anything. Then write one result that would count against the method.

TESTING.md
II

Observe

Run the practice frame. Notice which condition may be present in you before you assign it to the system. The contemplative categories are prompts, never scores, and never labels for other people.

PRACTICE-FRAME.md
III

Map

Read the four access questions: is it understandable, reachable, adaptable, traceable. Name the unit, the set, the denominator, and the time window before counting. Keep the four measures separate.

SCORECARD.md
IV

Trace

Run the extraction check on your own practice. Trace what leaves each contributor, where it accumulates, and what returns. Band consent, attribution, and value return separately. Do not average the bands.

EXTRACTION-CHECK.md
V

Adapt

Choose one small change from what the earlier stations surfaced. Record the baseline, the intended benefit, a plausible new harm, the consent it needs, and a review date. Then watch the cost and the result.

METHOD.md
VI

Return

Write up what happened with the field-test template. Cleared reports join the field-trial ledger as links, not endorsements. Private learning, refusal, and unmeasurable outcomes may end without a public report.

The evidence

PureLand has one maintainer-side, AI-assisted partial dry run and no independent field trial. The rights boundary

Four things that open projects often blur together. RIGHTS-AND-CONSENT.md holds the full guide, including the two-stage intake.

  • Repository license

    It grants reuse rights only for material the licensor has the authority to license.

  • Participant permission

    Consent must name the specific use. It does not become an open license unless the rights holder deliberately grants one.

  • Withdrawal

    Withdrawal can stop future uses that the project controls. It may not retrieve lawful copies already distributed, public archives, model weights, backups outside the project's control, or downstream adaptations.

  • Privacy and confidentiality

    Copyright permission does not settle privacy, confidentiality, publicity, data protection, or community authority.

Two readerships

A person and a model read this repository differently, and the difference is named rather than left to chance. The journey is the human lane, walked in order on one real practice at a body's pace. llms.txt is the agent lane, addressed to a model, and it states what a model must hand back to a person rather than complete. The crosswalk sets the two lanes against each other element by element. Station II has no agent counterpart, and that empty cell is a finding.

This page does not ask you to stay. Bring one real practice, a boundary, or a place where access and extraction pull against each other, and PureLand will scope what can be inspected, what must stay private, and what result would count against the method. Or do not. The record is here either way, and scroll position on this page earns you nothing.

When you're done reading, close the tab and go look at an actual horizon.
