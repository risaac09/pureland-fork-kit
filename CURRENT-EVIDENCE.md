# Current evidence

PureLand has one maintainer-side, AI-assisted partial dry run and no independent field trial. The record does not validate the method, establish a person-level outcome, or support a causal claim.

## Observed record

[FT-001](research/field-tests/ft-001-alchemy.md) applied the whole journey to seven public surfaces of the Alchemy repository. The assessor chose that boundary. No second reader participated. An AI agent performed the repository analysis, counting, and drafting. Human Observe was not performed. No affected user challenged the reading.

The dry run produced separate access and reciprocity readings, documented an intentional-deletion conflict, and selected a bounded adaptation. The Alchemy owner authorized and executed the adaptation through [Alchemy pull request 15](https://github.com/risaac09/alchemy/pull/15). Its follow-up window remains open through 2026-11-22.

## Assessor interpretation

The assessor interpreted the journey as adding some analytic value because it named tensions between provenance and designed ephemerality, treated decay as a possible cost of absence, and made the return path testable. This is an interpretation from inside the practice's orbit. It is not a confirmed finding.

## Competing explanations

- Alchemy's own documentation had already named two headline gaps, so ordinary repository review may explain much of the result.
- The extraction check, rather than the full journey or its sequence, may have produced the readings the assessor considered new.
- The seven-surface boundary was chosen after entering the practice. Another boundary would change every document-access denominator.
- The assessor worked for the owner and may have read ambiguous evidence generously.
- An AI design analysis cannot establish a walking person's attention sovereignty or substitute for human Observe.

## Absent evidence

- no human Observe record;
- no baseline and follow-up result from an affected person for stop, continue, question, correct, adapt, or refuse;
- no affected-user challenge, correction attempt, or refusal;
- no second reader or independence comparison;
- no completed follow-up on the executed adaptation;
- no person-level practical-agency result;
- no attention-sovereignty result;
- no causal identification; and
- no independent application, reliability estimate, validity result, benchmark, threshold, or certification.

## Current conclusion

FT-001 shows that the method can produce an inspectable partial record and can expose gaps in its own instrumentation. It cannot support H-PRIMARY-0.1 because required person-level outcome evidence is absent. Under the version 0.1 classification rule, the primary-hypothesis result is `unmeasurable` for this context. The assessor's sequencing interpretation is also unresolved.

## Next tests

1. Complete FT-001's follow-up on 2026-11-22 and record use, non-use, burden, and any correction or refusal without turning the change into a causal claim.
2. Run a human Observe station on a bounded practice with a predefined stop, question, correct, or refuse action.
3. Ask an affected person to test the correction or refusal route and record their objection, burden, and meaningful-return judgment.
4. Use a second reader who is independent of the practice and preserve category disagreements.
5. Run five to ten independent applications across at least three contexts before proposing reliability or validity work.
