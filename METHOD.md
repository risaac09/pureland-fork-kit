# Method

PureLand tests one bounded information practice at a time. The method asks whether a person can take named actions with more practical agency and attention sovereignty without increasing exposure, extractability, or burden for anyone the practice affects. The constructs are provisional. The instruments are not validated measures.

## Measurement unit

Every test records five different things. They must not be collapsed into one denominator.

1. **Walking person.** The person using the method and attempting the predefined actions. Record the person or role, their relationship to the practice, and whether the observation came from a human. An AI assessor is not a walking person for the human Observe station.
2. **Bounded information practice.** The encounter, workflow, system, or recurring way of handling information being tested. State the unit, the boundary, why that boundary was chosen, and one plausible alternative boundary.
3. **Affected people.** Name each person or group whose attention, information, labor, money, rights, or decision power may change. Record exposure, extractability, and shifted burden for each party separately.
4. **Artifact or surface set.** Name the complete set used for document-access counts, its denominator, exclusions, and the time window of the snapshot. This set supports access readings only.
5. **Observation window.** State when the action baseline was observed, when the adaptation begins, when follow-up ends, and the review date.

An artifact denominator cannot stand in for a person-level outcome. For example, `6/7 understandable surfaces` describes a document set. It does not show that a person gained practical agency or attention sovereignty.

## Test sequence

1. Record the tested hypothesis and the scope.
2. Define the five parts of the measurement unit above.
3. List the evidence available before analysis.
4. Write a disconfirming condition.
5. Predefine at least one concrete action: `stop`, `continue`, `question`, `correct`, `adapt`, or `refuse`. Record the baseline, the observation method, and the planned follow-up for each action.
6. Complete each in-scope station and record its status. The human Observe station cannot be inferred from system design or performed by an AI assessor.
7. Keep the four access readings and three reciprocity readings separate.
8. Record exposure, extractability, and burden for every affected party. Missing evidence stays `unmeasurable`.
9. Record disagreements, participant objections, permission, and rights review without converting absence into agreement.
10. Test one bounded adaptation. Record its intended benefit, possible new harm, observation window, review date, and follow-up.
11. Apply the classification rule in [HYPOTHESIS.md](HYPOTHESIS.md). Return a public-safe record, a private result, a refusal, or an unmeasurable result.

The structured requirements live in [the field-test schema](data/field-test.schema.json). The completion instructions live in [TESTING.md](TESTING.md) and [FIELD-TESTING.md](FIELD-TESTING.md).

## What the method does not establish

- A construct definition is not a validated measure.
- An access count is not a person-level outcome.
- An assessor interpretation is not an affected person's report.
- A completed form is not evidence that the hypothesis holds.
- A before-and-after impression does not establish causation.
- A profile is not a score, benchmark, threshold, certification, or moral verdict.
