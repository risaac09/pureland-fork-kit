# Rights and consent

This guide separates four things that open projects often collapse.

## Repository license

The repository license grants reuse rights only for material the licensor has authority to license. It is irrevocable for copies already received under its terms.

## Participant permission

Consent governs collection, processing, retention, and publication of participant material. It must name the use. It does not become an open license unless the rights holder deliberately grants one.

## Withdrawal

Withdrawal can stop future controlled uses. It may not retrieve lawful copies already distributed, public archives, model weights, backups outside the project's control, or downstream adaptations. State these limits before collection. Record the steps actually taken after withdrawal.

## Privacy and confidentiality

Copyright permission does not settle privacy, confidentiality, publicity, data protection, or community authority. An open license is not a privacy waiver.

## Minimum operating rules

- Keep participant material out of the public repository.
- Collect only what the stated purpose requires.
- Name every third-party processor and its retention terms.
- Obtain separate permission for AI processing and model development.
- Set a deletion or review date before collection.
- Preserve a protected record of current permission without preserving unnecessary disclosure.
- Escalate legal questions to qualified counsel in the relevant jurisdiction.
