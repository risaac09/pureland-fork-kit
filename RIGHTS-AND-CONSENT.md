# Rights and consent

This guide separates four things that open projects often blur together.

## Repository license

The repository license grants reuse rights only for material the licensor actually has the authority to license. For copies already received under its terms, it cannot be taken back.

## Participant permission

Consent governs the collection, processing, retention, and publication of participant material. It must name the specific use. It does not become an open license unless the rights holder deliberately grants one.

## Withdrawal

Withdrawal can stop future uses that the project controls. It may not retrieve lawful copies already distributed, public archives, model weights, backups outside the project's control, or downstream adaptations. State these limits before collecting anything. Record the steps actually taken after a withdrawal.

## Privacy and confidentiality

Copyright permission does not settle privacy, confidentiality, publicity, data protection, or community authority. An open license is not a privacy waiver.

## Minimum operating rules

- Keep participant material out of the public repository.
- Collect only what the stated purpose requires.
- Name every third-party processor and its retention terms.
- Get separate permission for AI processing and for model development.
- Set a deletion or review date before collecting.
- Keep a protected record of current permissions without keeping unnecessary disclosures.
- Take legal questions to qualified counsel in the relevant jurisdiction.

Use [templates/consent-register.md](templates/consent-register.md) as a decision record, not as universal legal language.
