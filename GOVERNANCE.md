# Governance

Isaac Rubinstein is the initial maintainer and holds merge and release authority. This is sole-maintainer governance. It is not shared governance, even when contributors advise, review, or disagree. The concentration of power remains a project risk.

## Decision record

Material changes should state:

- the problem and the affected people;
- the evidence and what is missing;
- the consent, rights, privacy, and extraction risks;
- the dissenting views;
- the decision, its owner, and the reasons for it; and
- the decision date and review date.

## Participant and contributor rights

Sole maintainership does not give the maintainer authority over another person's material. Under this project's operating rules:

- a participant may veto an artifact containing their own material;
- an affected person may attach visible dissent to a public interpretation when publication is otherwise permitted;
- any affected person may request correction or takedown of a record the project controls;
- a contributor whose method change is rejected receives a public reason, unless publishing the reason would expose protected material; and
- when feasible, the maintainer and affected person choose an independent reviewer together for a disputed rights or closure decision.

These rights do not grant one person authority over community-held knowledge or another person's material. They do not promise removal from copies, archives, backups, model weights, or adaptations the project does not control. [RIGHTS-AND-CONSENT.md](RIGHTS-AND-CONSENT.md) defines the review and withdrawal record.

## Merge criteria

A change should improve clarity, evidence, contestability, or participant agency without expanding collection or exposure without review. Research claims need sources. PureLand synthesis must be labeled as synthesis. A field report needs a documented public-safe review. Changes to the Buddhist frame must keep source material distinct from PureLand's synthesis.

Participant refusal and private learning are valid outcomes. The absence of a public report does not make a field test a failure. The maintainer must not merge participant material without approval of the actual artifact.

## Release authority

The maintainer may merge after review and automated checks. The maintainer may reject a technically valid change when it exposes people, creates an unsupported claim, or exceeds the contributor's authority. The maintainer gives a public reason unless the reason itself must remain private, in which case the public record states that a protected reason exists and names a review date.

## Disputes, correction, and closure

Preserve objections in the decision record. Distinguish disagreement with an interpretation from refusal to publish material. Do not rewrite disagreement as consensus.

For a correction, takedown, disputed rights decision, or disputed closure:

1. Stop any new release the maintainer controls when the dispute concerns publication authority.
2. Record the request without exposing the protected material.
3. Identify the person or community with authority over the disputed material.
4. Choose an independent reviewer with the affected person when feasible.
5. Record the decision, reasons, actions, remaining limits, and review date.

The maintainer retains the final repository decision while sole maintainership continues. The artifact veto for a person's own material still applies.

## Governance review condition

The project will review its governance when evidence shows that sole maintainership no longer gives affected people a credible route to contest decisions or maintain the work. Evidence may include repeated unresolved rights disputes, sustained review or maintenance work by people other than the maintainer, recurring participant or community decisions that need independent authority, or correction and takedown requests that one maintainer cannot handle promptly.

The review may recommend more maintainers, a standing independent reviewer, a community review group, or another structure. Any threshold or structure adopted at that point is a provisional operating choice subject to a dated review. Acceptance of a contribution does not automatically earn governance authority.

## Conduct and repair

No harassment, coercion, doxxing, identity-based attacks, or public release of private material. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for confidential reports. Public issues are the right place for method disputes that can be discussed without protected material. When a dispute needs protected detail, request a private review channel without describing that detail.
