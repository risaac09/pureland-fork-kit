# Governance

Isaac Rubinstein is the sole maintainer and holds merge and release authority. This governance structure is sole maintainership. That concentration of power is a project risk, not a mature commons.

## Decision record

Material changes should state:

- the problem and the affected people;
- the evidence, and the evidence that is missing;
- the consent, privacy, and extraction risks;
- the dissenting views;
- the decision, its owner, and a review date.

## Merge criteria

A change should improve clarity, evidence, contestability, or participant agency without quietly expanding what gets collected or exposed. Research claims need sources. Field reports need an artifact-specific public-safe review under [RIGHTS-AND-CONSENT.md](RIGHTS-AND-CONSENT.md). Changes to the Buddhist frame must keep the source material distinct from PureLand's own synthesis.

## Release authority

The maintainer may merge after review and automated checks. The maintainer may reject a technically valid change when it exposes people or creates an unsupported claim. The maintainer must give a public reason for rejecting a proposed method change, without disclosing protected material.

## Rights that do not depend on merge authority

Sole maintainership does not give the maintainer authority over another person's material or community-held knowledge. The project recognizes these rights:

- **Artifact veto:** an affected person may refuse publication of that person's own material. A no or not-yet decision blocks publication by the project.
- **Visible dissent:** when publication is permitted but an affected person disputes the project's interpretation, the project must invite that person to provide or approve the wording of dissent attached to the interpretation. The project does not present disagreement as consensus.
- **Correction and takedown requests:** any affected person may request correction or removal of a record the project controls. The project must preserve the request, respond with the action taken, and state any limit it cannot control.
- **Reasons for method decisions:** a contributor whose method change is rejected receives a public reason that separates source evidence, project synthesis, rights limits, and maintainer judgment.
- **Independent review:** for a disputed rights determination or a decision to close a correction or takedown request, the maintainer must seek a reviewer chosen with the affected person when feasible. The reviewer should have no material role in the disputed decision.

While a rights review is unresolved, the project does not publish the affected person's material or begin a new use. Share only the minimum private record needed with the reviewer. The reviewer records a recommendation and its basis. The maintainer retains repository merge and release authority but cannot override an affected person's artifact veto.

If the parties cannot identify an appropriate reviewer or private review channel, record that limit without exposing the dispute. Keep participant material unpublished and stop new project-controlled uses until the rights basis is clear.

## Review of the governance structure

Accepted contributions do not automatically confer governance rights. The project will review its governance structure when the decision record shows that concentrated authority has caused an unresolved rights conflict, repeated closure disputes, or a recurring evidence-backed method dispute that the current process cannot fairly resolve. A review may also begin before assigning any new person authority over rights reviews, merges, or releases.

The review must use the documented cases, seek input from the affected people, consider conflicts of interest and actual workload, and record what authority remains concentrated. Contributor count alone is not evidence that a governance change is ready or legitimate.

## Conduct and repair

No harassment, coercion, doxxing, identity-based attacks, or public release of private material. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for confidential reports. Public issues are the right place for public-safe method disputes, not for confidential records, participant material, or protected community knowledge.
